## Is project ka asli objective kya hai?

Company abhi ye sab use karti hai:

* WhatsApp
* Excel
* Word
* Calls
* Founder ki memory

Isliye bahut mistakes hoti hain.

ERP banne ke baad:

* Sab kuch ek jagah hoga.
* Har stage ka owner hoga.
* Payment bina production nahi.
* QC bina packing nahi.
* Ready Size confirm bina stitching nahi.
* Sabka audit trail rahega. 

## Backend Developer ke hisaab se tujhe kya banana hai?

Ye ERP roughly in modules me divide hoga:

1. Authentication & Role Management
2. CRM (Lead Management)
3. Customer & Project Management
4. Site Visit & Measurements
5. BOQ / Estimation
6. Proposal & Quotation
7. Pricing Master
8. Order Activation
9. Design & Drawings
10. Procurement & Vendors
11. Inventory
12. Production Tracking
13. Quality Check (QC)
14. Dispatch & Packing
15. Installation
16. Payments
17. Rework / Maintenance
18. Reports & Dashboard
19. Notifications
20. Settings / Masters

Ye FRD total **42 business stages ko 10 major modules me organize karta hai**, aur wahi tumhare ERP ka blueprint hai.
